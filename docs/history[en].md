# Game Story

Jack, an ordinary boy strolling through the city, was suddenly plunged into darkness. An evil dragon had stolen the torch that illuminated the planet. The boy was stunned. "What am I going to do?" he thought. A deep voice told him:

"Retrieve the torch, or the city will be without light forever!"

To retrieve the torch, you need to find the iron key; without it, you will not be able to open the door to the dragon's chamber.

# Scene Map (~8 scenes, 2 endings)

## Scene 1: The Cave Entrance

- Enter through the left tunnel -> Scene 2

- Enter through the right tunnel -> Scene 3

## Scene 2: The Dark Tunnel

- Take the iron key and continue -> Scene 4

- Ignore the key and continue -> Scene 4

- (The difference only appears in Scene 5)

## Scene 3: The Rotten Bridge

- Run across -> Ending 1: You fall (defeat)

- Go back and choose the other tunnel -> Scene 2

## Scene 4: The Guardian (a creature blocking the path)

- Fight -> Scene 5

- Try to sneak past -> Ending 1: Defeat (the creature kills you)

## Scene 5: The Chamber Door

- If you have the key: select "Open the door" -> Scene 6

- If you don't have the key Key: The "Back" option only appears -> Scene 2 (To obtain the key)

## Scene 6: The Dragon

- Steal the torch while it sleeps -> Ending 2: Easy Victory
- Wake the dragon and negotiate -> Ending 3: Secret Victory
