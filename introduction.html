History

Jack was just a boy walking through the city when, suddenly, all the lights went out. A darkness fell over the place

A dragon had stolen the magic torch that illuminated the planet

Frightened and not knowing what to do, Jack heard a deep voice coming from somewhere unknown:

Retrieve the torch, or the city will be forever without light!

To reach the dragon, Jack needs to find the iron key that opens the door to his chamber

During the journey, he will face dangerous paths, a mysterious guardian

And decisions that could change the fate of his adventure
